feat(RichTextEditor): `linkPlugin({ look })` takes `marginTop` and `marginBottom` for button links, and a box for text links: `paddingTop`, `paddingRight`, `paddingBottom`, `paddingLeft`, `backgroundColor`, `borderColor`, `borderWidth`, `borderStyle` and `borderRadius`. A field left out keeps Fondue's look, so links look the same until a host sets one.
